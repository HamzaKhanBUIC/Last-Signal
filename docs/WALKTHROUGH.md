## 🏃 SURVIVAL MECHANICS & NEXUS-9 EVASION

### 1. Acoustic Footstep Footprints
- **Sprinting (`Shift`)**: Emits loud acoustic vibrations ($300\text{ px}$ radius). If NEXUS-9 is in adjacent rooms, it will immediately transition to `INVESTIGATE` and converge on your position.
- **Walking (`WASD`)**: Moderate sound profile ($100\text{ px}$ radius). Safe for general traversal when NEXUS-9 is far.
- **Crouching / Stealth (`Ctrl` / `C`)**: Complete silence ($0\text{ px}$ acoustic radius). Use when navigating close to NEXUS-9 patrol waypoints.

### 2. Line of Sight & Flashlight Discipline
- Hiding behind bulkheads, doors, and server racks occludes NEXUS-9's $110^\circ$ vision cone.
- Shining your tactical flashlight (`F`) directly onto NEXUS-9's sensor cluster triples its visual awareness speed and initiates an instant `CHASE`. Toggle flashlight OFF when in the same chamber!

### 3. Proximity Telemetry & ECG Monitor
- Keep an eye on your **ECG Heartbeat line** and listen to the dynamic sub-bass drone. An accelerating heartbeat and CRT chromatic distortion signify NEXUS-9 is within $<250\text{ px}$.
